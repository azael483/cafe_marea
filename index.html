Questions we hear a lot

Quick answers before you come by.

Do you have vegan options?

Yes. We offer oat and almond milk at no extra cost, and two vegan pastries every day.

Can I work here all day?

Yes. Every seat has a power outlet and the Wi-Fi is free. We only ask that you order something every couple of hours.

Do you take card payments?

We accept cash and all major cards. We do not take reservations, tables are first come, first served.

Can I order for an event?

Yes. Send us a message with the date and number of guests, and we'll reply within one business day.
